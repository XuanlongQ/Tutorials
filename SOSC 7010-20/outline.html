Seminar: Computational Social Science

SOSC 7010-20 · 2022 Fall – Present

This seminar is a required course for students in computational social science, including CSS fellows, students in the CSS concentration, and students engaged in CSS studies.
🔗 Lab Website: CSSL (Computational Social Science Lab)

🌟 About the Seminar

  • 🧪 Lab — The first lab in Hong Kong advocating for computational social science.
  • 🗓️ Rhythm — Every two weeks, we gather to discuss and exchange ideas.
  • 📚 Anchor — Students replicate and extend influential work published in top journals.
  • 🧭 Repository — Records the papers we have replicated and traces the path we have taken.

What we do
